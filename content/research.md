My research interests lie at the intersection of biological oceanography, marine imaging, and scientific computing.

## Marine Imaging

I work with in situ imaging systems to observe plankton, particles, and other organisms in the marine environment.

## Scientific Computing

I use image processing, machine learning, and computational workflows to analyze large oceanographic imaging datasets.

## Field Oceanography

My field experience includes supporting oceanographic research at sea, including scientific instrumentation, sampling operations, and the deployment and recovery of oceanographic equipment.
