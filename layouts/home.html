Hannah Kepner

I am a biological oceanographer interested in marine imaging, instrumentation, and computational approaches to studying plankton and marine ecosystems.

My work combines in situ imaging with scientific computing and data analysis to better understand marine organisms and their environment.

Much of my research has taken place at sea, working with oceanographic instrumentation and imaging systems in the Gulf of Alaska, California Current, and North Sea.
