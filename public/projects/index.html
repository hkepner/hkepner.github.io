Projects

My work spans biological oceanography, marine imaging, scientific computing, and the development and application of oceanographic observing technologies.

Current Projects

Icy Seas Co-Laboratory

At the Icy Seas Co-Laboratory, I work at the intersection of marine science, in situ imaging, and scientific computing. My work focuses on developing and supporting tools for the collection, processing, and analysis of oceanographic imaging data.

Current work includes the curation of plankton image datasets, development of image-analysis workflows, and the use of machine learning to improve the detection and segmentation of organisms and particles in marine imagery.

Pelagia

Pelagia is an open-source platform for managing and analyzing marine imaging data.

My work with Pelagia focuses on image processing and data workflows for plankton and particle imagery, including the development and evaluation of methods for ROI detection, segmentation, refinement, and classification.

Previous Projects

Northern Gulf of Alaska Long-Term Ecological Research (NGA LTER)

The Northern Gulf of Alaska Long-Term Ecological Research program investigates the physical and biological processes that support the productivity and resilience of the Northern Gulf of Alaska ecosystem.

My work with the NGA LTER involved biological oceanography and long-term ecosystem observations in the Gulf of Alaska, including the collection and analysis of plankton and oceanographic data.

Monitoring and Research for Nature Enhancement and Species Protection (MONS)

MONS is a large-scale research and monitoring program investigating ecological change in the Dutch North Sea, including the effects of offshore development, fisheries, and other environmental pressures.

My work contributed to zooplankton monitoring in the Dutch North Sea, with a focus on the role of zooplankton in the food web, utilizing imaging, molecular ecology, stable isotopes, and traditional taxonomic analysis.
